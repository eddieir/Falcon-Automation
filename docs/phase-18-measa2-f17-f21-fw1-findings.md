**Consequence (owner rule: worker WebSocket traffic must be contained or must cause a fail-closed refusal; it is not excluded from the security claims).** On the pinned build the worker WebSocket is not contained. The design therefore may not claim that customer-bound traffic from workers is observed or refused; it needs either a measured containment mechanism or a fail-closed refusal condition. No containment mechanism has been measured. Candidate to measure next (unmeasured, not a finding): a `Content-Security-Policy` `connect-src` header added when Falcon fulfils the document and worker-script responses through its routing handler, since dedicated worker scripts were shown to be routed (SPIKE-W). Until a mechanism is measured, the fail-closed position is that execute mode does not arm.

**Limits.** Dedicated, blob and nested workers only; shared and service worker sockets, `WebTransport`, `EventSource` and `fetch` from workers were not measured here (worker `fetch` is routed per SPIKE-W). Linux and GitHub-runner behaviour unmeasured.
