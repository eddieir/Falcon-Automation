## Where Falcon sits

Falcon is an open-source, self-hosted Playwright framework with scoped, locally validated, human-approved healing (docs/PHASE-PLANS.md, "Forward strategy"). It overlaps most with the developer-first and open-source healing categories. It does not compete on hosted infrastructure, device clouds, enterprise administration or compliance attestations, and Phase 18 does not change that.
